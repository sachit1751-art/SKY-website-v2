🛠️ Recovery Flashing Guide

Offline steps for TWRP / OrangeFox / Sky Recovery:

1. Reboot to Fastboot (Vol Down + Power)
2. fastboot flash recovery recovery.img
3. fastboot boot recovery.img
4. Format Data (Type 'yes')
5. ADB Sideload filename.zip
